## 🎯 Three Paths to Google Scholar

Choose based on your goals:

### **Path 1: Zenodo (Recommended)** ⭐⭐⭐
**Best for:** Most people
**Time:** 15 minutes setup + 1-2 weeks indexing
**Effort:** Low
**Result:** Official DOI + Auto-tracking

**Steps:**
1. Link GitHub to Zenodo
2. Create release
3. Get DOI
4. Wait for Google Scholar

### **Path 2: JOSS** ⭐⭐
**Best for:** Academic prestige
**Time:** 2-4 weeks review
**Effort:** Medium (write paper, respond to reviews)
**Result:** Peer-reviewed paper + DOI + Immediate indexing

**Steps:**
1. Write `paper.md`
2. Add tests
3. Submit to JOSS
4. Get published

### **Path 3: arXiv** ⭐
**Best for:** Quick visibility
**Time:** 3-7 days
**Effort:** Low (write 2-4 page report)
**Result:** Preprint + Fast indexing

**Steps:**
1. Write technical report
2. Submit to arXiv
3. Get arXiv ID
4. Appears on Google Scholar
